Executive Briefing

99 Happy Valley Rd
Acquisition Strategy

Prepared for: Kristen Derk

Stoneside Ventures, LLC dba Stoneside Retreats

Hi Kristen. This is the executive summary for the short term rental project at 99 Happy Valley Road in Elysburg. Here is exactly how we plan to acquire, renovate, and launch the property by May 2027.

1. The Acquisition Strategy

Target Offer: $10,000 to $12,000 Cash

The sellers received an appraisal for eighty-two thousand dollars. However, that appraisal assumes they can sell the cabin on the open market. Because we own the land, our lease terms give us total control over approving any new buyer. Due to the severe flood risk in FEMA Flood Zone AE, we will not issue a new lease to an outside buyer.

Since the cabin is uninsurable against floods, any money we invest could be washed away. Therefore, ten to twelve thousand dollars represents our absolute maximum risk tolerance. If they decline our cash offer, their lease requires them to pay thousands of dollars out of pocket to demolish and remove the structure. We are offering them the best financial way out.

2. Remodel & Operations

  • Flood-Resilient Renovation: We will remove all carpet and install waterproof Luxury Vinyl Plank flooring. We will use mold-resistant drywall on the lower walls and elevate the electrical systems.
  • Smart Furnishing: We will buy durable used wood and metal furniture from Facebook Marketplace, but invest in brand new memory foam mattresses and commercial-grade white linens that can be bleached.
  • Remote Management: The property will be fully automated with smart locks and thermostats. We will manage bookings from Atlanta, utilizing local cleaners and a handyman dispatched via our existing Jobber software.
  • Seasonality: To avoid frozen pipes and high heating bills, we will operate strictly during the summer season targeting Knoebels visitors, and fully winterize the cabin from October through April.

3. The Financial Outlook

The total cash required to buy, remodel, and launch the property is estimated between twenty-one thousand and twenty-eight thousand dollars.

4. The Compound Vision

This cabin is just Phase 1. Our long-term vision is to transition the adjacent family-controlled parcels into a multi-unit outdoor hospitality destination:

Lot 99

We will subdivide the remaining space to rent out an RV pad for guests bringing their own travel trailers, complete with a hitching post.

Lot 97

We will develop up to three premium campsites, leveraging the existing outhouse and adding wooden decks or concrete slabs for tents.

Lot 95

We will cosmetically refresh the existing motorhome for turnkey rentals, and replace the broken secondary camper with a high-ROI travel trailer.
